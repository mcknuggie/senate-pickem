Polls: PollResults.org (data from The New York Times polls page, CC BY 4.0), loaded live each time the game opens. Polling averages are calculated here: the latest poll from each pollster in the three weeks before the newest poll.

Odds: Polymarket US, loaded live. Each figure is the midpoint between the current buy and sell price, roughly the market's implied chance of winning. If live data can't be reached, the game shows a snapshot from Oct 21, 2026.

Nominees: Office of the Clerk, U.S. House (Oct 8, 2026). Candidate notes summarize public reporting and Wikipedia race pages and were written in October 2026. Candidate characters are 16-bit caricatures drawn from public photos (mostly Wikipedia portraits). They are approximate, drawn in one shared style, and not endorsed by any campaign.
